ARAM Mayhem Overlay
===================

Grade badges above your augment cards, plus a small panel for builds.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Champ select: the panel opens on your champion.
2. Game starts: the panel hides.
3. Augment pick: a badge appears above each card.
     S = Great   A = Good   B = Okay   C = Weak   D = Skip
   Green = best of the cards shown.
4. You pick: the badges disappear.

TWO KEYS
F9    Panel in / out
F10   Badges in / out (also reads the cards again)
Change either key: click it at the bottom of the panel,
then press the key you want. Esc cancels.

COMBOS AND NICHE BUILDS
If a card is part of a known combo for your champion, the badge
gets a label on top:
  God combo / Strong combo   Take it
  Niche tech / Fun build     Off-meta, for fun games
  Trap / Bugged              Looks good, is not
  "with X"                   The other augment the combo needs
  "Build X"                  The item this augment needs
The Augments tab lists every combo for your champion.

WHAT THE GRADE MEANS
Letter       How high U.GG ranks that augment for YOUR champion.
"% pick it"  How often players pick it overall (arammayhem.com).
Combos come from arammayhem.com's combo list.
There is no public win rate for Mayhem augments.

UPDATES
The app checks GitHub each time it starts and updates itself.
The version is shown at the bottom right of the panel.
Close and reopen the app to get the newest version.

SETUP
1. Unzip anywhere. Delete older versions first.
2. Open "ARAM Mayhem Overlay.exe".
   Windows may warn: click "More info" > "Run anyway".
3. League: Borderless mode, English, on your main monitor.

Screen reading uses Windows' built-in text recognition, only when
a pick is due and you are dead. Images stay on your PC.
